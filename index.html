Lumina AI
AI-Powered Business Analytics Platform

Businesses collect a lot of sales and customer data, but turning it into decisions usually needs a data team — dashboards are rigid, forecasting is hard, and data often sits disconnected in an ERP. Lumina AI is a SaaS platform that brings it together: a live dashboard with KPI cards and interactive charts, AI-generated insights that summarize the data, an AI chat/agent that answers questions in plain language and filters charts by city or product, sales forecasting with linear regression, secure login with bcrypt and JWT, and an in-progress Odoo integration to sync sales and customer data.

RIS — Hospital & Radiology Management
Al-Safwa Hospital — Management System

Hospitals and radiology centers often run reception, billing, doctors' schedules, and inventory on separate tools or on paper — leading to double-booked exam slots, payments that don't match bookings, and no single view of a patient or invoice. I built a role-based system covering the full patient journey: five roles (Admin, Reception, Accountant, Doctor, Store Keeper), a patient portal with booking, time-slot management so slots can't overlap, payments and invoicing linked to each booking, a doctor view with a completion flow that frees the slot, and a back-office database covering employees, salaries, attendance, inventory, and expenses across 16+ tables.

Static E-Commerce Website
